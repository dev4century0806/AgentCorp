Terms of Use

Use AgentCorp lawfully and only with accounts, repositories, tickets, and services you are authorized to access. You are responsible for your account, the instructions you give workers, and reviewing agent-generated work before using or shipping it.

Workers use the coding-agent accounts and third-party services you connect. Their availability, limits, and terms may apply separately. We may change or discontinue features, and may suspend access when needed to protect the service or its users.

Privacy

AgentCorp records local usage on your account:

  • event names, setup choices, counts, and timestamps
  • usage is stored locally

It does not record message text, ticket titles, or repository paths. All operations are isolated to your local environment.

We may update these terms as the product changes. Continued use after an update means you accept the updated terms.
